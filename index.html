Trees spread fire probabilistically according to neighbouring cells, while growth, lightning and wind continuously alter the system.

How it works

Each position is a cell with a state such as empty, tree, burning or burnt. On every generation the automaton checks neighbouring cells and applies the selected probabilities. Trees near fire may ignite, empty cells may grow new trees, lightning can create independent fires, and wind biases fire spread in a chosen direction.
